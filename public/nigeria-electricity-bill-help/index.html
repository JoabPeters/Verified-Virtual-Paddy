How this service works

Nigeria electricity customers can use Verified Virtual Paddy to find the official customer-service and self-service channels of their electricity distribution company. Select your state and distribution company on our directory, then continue to the provider's own website or contact channel to request an authentic bill, payment history, meter information or billing support.

Important service limitation

The platform is an independent information directory. It does not issue electricity bills, access DisCo customer databases, verify meter ownership or guarantee provider responses. Only the relevant authorised electricity provider can issue or confirm its official bill.

About Verified Virtual Paddy

Verified Virtual Paddy is a free, independent digital assistance project. It helps users navigate official information and prepare clearly labelled customer-supplied documents. It is not a government agency, regulator, bank or electricity distribution company.
