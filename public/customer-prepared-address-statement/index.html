Important service limitation

This statement is self-declared. Verified Virtual Paddy does not independently inspect or verify the address. It is not an electricity bill, utility invoice, government-issued document or certified proof of address. Ask the organisation requesting proof of address whether a customer-prepared statement is acceptable before relying on it.
